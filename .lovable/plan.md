# Improve answer pattern

## What will change
- Keep the exact direct answer first, then give each developed answer a clear topic heading.
- Structure explanations as: rule, application to the question or scenario, approach/steps, and a concise conclusion.
- Make answers slightly more explanatory without adding filler, unsupported assumptions, or repeated points.
- Preserve short answers for simple factual questions and the existing source, precision, and calculation safeguards.
